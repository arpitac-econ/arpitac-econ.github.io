Arpita Chatterjee

Senior Economist, International Finance Division, Federal Reserve Board

I am a Senior Economist at the Federal Reserve Board, where I work on energy, commodity markets and trade prices. Before joining the Board in 2024, I was an Associate Professor of Economics at UNSW Sydney and at the Indian Institute of Management Bangalore. I received my PhD in Economics from Princeton University in 2010.

My research is in international economics and macroeconomics, with a strong applied-econometrics component. I study how macroeconomic and trade policies work in advanced and emerging economies and how they interact with globalization.
